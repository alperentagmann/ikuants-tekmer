# İKÜANTS TEKMER — SUPER ADMIN & OPERATIONS USER GUIDE

## 1. Introduction

The İKÜANTS TEKMER Admin Platform serves as the central Digital Operating System for university staff, program directors, mentor coordinators, and institutional leadership.

---

## 2. Navigating the Workspace

### 2.1. Dashboard & Management Intelligence
- **Main Dashboard (`/admin`)**: Real-time institutional KPIs, incubation statistics, pending applications, active tasks, and system health status.
- **Benim Günüm / My Day (`/admin/benim-gunum`)**: Tailored personal workspace aggregating daily todos, assigned tasks, scheduled meetings, overdue deadlines, and recent activity.

### 2.2. Work Management & Operations
- **Task Studio & Kanban (`/admin/gorevler`)**: Trello/Jira-grade task management with custom stages (Yapılacak, Devam Ediyor, Kontrol Bekliyor, Tamamlandı), priority badges, checklists, subtasks, and drag-and-drop state persistence.
- **Unified Calendar (`/admin/takvim`)**: Multi-category view for mentoring sessions, training workshops, project milestones, and meetings.
- **Meeting Management (`/admin/toplantilar`)**: Agenda tracking, participant invites, minutes recording, and one-click conversion from decisions to actionable tasks.
- **Activity & Evidence Log (`/admin/faaliyetler`)**: Official activity logging with participant lists, cost tracking, photos, and institutional reporting tags.

### 2.3. CRM & Stakeholders
- **Application Pipeline (`/admin/basvurular`)**: Multi-stage pipeline (Yeni, Ön İnceleme, Değerlendirme, Görüşme, Kabul, Ret) with scoring, internal notes, and applicant notification triggers.
- **Entrepreneur CRM (`/admin/girisimciler`)**: Startup portfolio management, founders, patents, grants, funding history, and public visibility toggle.
- **Mentor Network (`/admin/mentorler`)**: Mentor profiles, areas of expertise, program assignments, and session hour logs.
- **Stakeholder Directory (`/admin/rehber`)**: Comprehensive directory of university academics, corporate partners, investors, and suppliers with duplicate detection.

### 2.4. Public Website CMS & Studios
- **Homepage Studio (`/admin/anasayfa`)**: Visual section hierarchy editor, block reordering, slide activation, and multi-device preview.
- **Hero & Slider Studio (`/admin/hero-slider`)**: Complete management of all 23 hero slides, headlines, CTA links, and image assignments.
- **Page & Block Builder (`/admin/sayfalar`)**: Block-based custom page creation with rich text, media, accordion, grid, and contact blocks.
- **Navigation Builder (`/admin/menu`)**: Drag-and-drop menu structure management with dropdown nesting.
- **News & Announcements (`/admin/haberler`)**: Editorial publishing workflow (Draft, In Review, Approved, Published).
- **Form Builder (`/admin/form-builder`)**: Dynamic form schema builder with 16 field types, validation rules, and KVKK consent toggles.
- **Media Library (`/admin/medya`)**: Centralized asset manager with folders, tags, search, and secure upload sanitization.

### 2.5. Reporting & BI
- **Reports Center (`/admin/raporlar`)**: Automated daily, monthly, and annual operational intelligence reports with PDF and Excel export.

### 2.6. System & Security
- **User & Role Management (`/admin/kullanicilar`)**: Invite new team members, manage RBAC permissions, revoke sessions, and monitor activity.
- **Security Center (`/admin/guvenlik`)**: Multi-Factor Authentication (TOTP) setup, brute-force logs, and PII masking audits.
- **Integrations (`/admin/entegrasyonlar`)**: Status monitor for M365, Meta, Email, KBS, and Redis.
